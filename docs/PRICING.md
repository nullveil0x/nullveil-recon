## 1. Community (free)

**MIT License** — free of charge.

- Personal, hobby, educational, and open-source use.
- Internal **evaluation** by a business (time-boxed, non-production).
- Full feature set of the open-source tool.
- Community support via GitHub issues.

## 2. Pro / Supporter

**$29–$99 / month** (individual) · **$299–$999 / month** (team)

- Everything in Community.
- Priority issue triage.
- Early access to pre-release modules.
- A commercial-use grant up to a stated scope (seats/targets) — see the
  Commercial License for the exact grant.

> Launch channels: **GitHub Sponsors**, **Polar.sh**, **Gumroad**.

## 3. Consulting / Deliverable

**$500–$5,000+ per engagement** (scope-dependent)

- You use NULLVEIL Recon as the delivery engine for a paid assessment.
- Includes planning, scan execution, analysis, and a client-ready report
  (HTML/PDF via the built-in executive exporter).
- See [`CONSULTING.md`](CONSULTING.md) for the service wrapper.

## 4. Team / Self-Hosted

**$500–$2,500 / seat / year**

- Named seats for a security team.
- Internal use across the team's engagements.
- Priority support + update channel.

## 5. OEM / White-Label

**Negotiated**

- Embed or rebrand NULLVEIL Recon inside your product or managed service.
- Revenue-share or flat-fee models available.

## 6. Enterprise

**Negotiated**

- Unlimited internal seats.
- SLA + priority support.
- Custom integrations (SIEM export, SSO, on-prem deployment).
- Security review / questionnaire support.

**Do I need a license to try it?** No — evaluation is free.

commercial use → Commercial License (see the Consulting/Deliverable tier).

**Is the Community version crippled?** No. The open-source tool is fully
featured; the paid tiers add legal rights, support, and commercial terms.

**I'm a student / researcher.** Community (MIT) covers you.
